## Chapter 1: Nature and Significance of Management (10 Qs)

**Q1.** Management is considered a science because:
(A) It has universally accepted principles
(B) It involves systematic body of knowledge
(C) Principles are derived through observation and experimentation
(D) All of the above
**Answer: D** — Management has all features of science but is not an exact science.

**Q2.** "Management is getting things done through and with people." This definition was given by:
(A) Henri Fayol (B) Peter Drucker (C) F.W. Taylor (D) Harold Koontz
**Answer: D**

**Q3.** Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of management?
(A) Goal-oriented process (B) Pervasive (C) Tangible force (D) Multi-dimensional
**Answer: C** — Management is an intangible force, not tangible.

**Q4.** At which level of management are operational decisions primarily made?
(A) Top level (B) Middle level (C) Lower/Supervisory level (D) All levels equally
**Answer: C**

**Q5.** Coordination is considered:
(A) A separate function of management (B) The essence of management
(C) Only important at top level (D) Same as controlling
**Answer: B** — Coordination is the essence of management, not a separate function.

**Q6.** Management as a profession does NOT fully satisfy which criterion?
(A) Well-defined body of knowledge (B) Restricted entry
(C) Professional association (D) Service motive
**Answer: B** — No minimum qualification is prescribed to become a manager.

**Q7.** Effectiveness in management refers to:
(A) Completing the task with minimum resources (B) Completing the task on time
(C) Achieving organizational goals (D) Reducing employee turnover
**Answer: C** — Effectiveness = doing the right things (achieving goals).

**Q8.** Efficiency in management means:
(A) Achieving goals regardless of cost (B) Getting maximum output with minimum input
(C) Completing work before deadline (D) Hiring the best employees
**Answer: B**

**Q9.** The three dimensions of management are:
(A) Planning, organizing, controlling (B) Management of work, people, and operations
(C) Top, middle, and lower management (D) Science, art, and profession
**Answer: B**

**Q10.** A manager at the middle level is primarily responsible for:
(A) Formulating overall organizational strategy (B) Implementing policies and plans
(C) Supervising daily operations (D) Interacting with customers
**Answer: B**

## Chapter 2: Principles of Management (10 Qs)

**Q11.** Taylor's "differential piece wage system" is aimed at:
(A) Motivating efficient workers (B) Providing equal pay to all workers
(C) Reducing wage disputes (D) Eliminating time study
**Answer: A** — Efficient workers get higher piece rate, inefficient get lower.

**Q12.** The principle of "Unity of Command" was given by:
(A) F.W. Taylor (B) Henri Fayol (C) Elton Mayo (D) Max Weber
**Answer: B**

**Q13.** "Science, not rule of thumb" is a principle of:
(A) Henri Fayol (B) F.W. Taylor (C) Peter Drucker (D) Elton Mayo
**Answer: B** — Taylor advocated replacing rule of thumb with scientific methods.

**Q14.** Which of Fayol's principles states that authority and responsibility should go hand in hand?
(A) Unity of direction (B) Scalar chain (C) Authority and responsibility (D) Discipline
**Answer: C**

**Q15.** Fayol's principle of "Esprit de Corps" means:
(A) Division of work (B) Team spirit and unity (C) Equity among workers (D) Centralization
**Answer: B** — "Union is strength" — promote team spirit.

**Q16.** "Motion Study" in Scientific Management involves:
(A) Studying time taken for each task (B) Studying unnecessary movements in work
(C) Studying worker fatigue (D) Studying organizational structure
**Answer: B** — Eliminating unnecessary motions to improve efficiency.

**Q17.** The "Gang Plank" is an exception to which principle?
(A) Unity of command (B) Scalar chain (C) Unity of direction (D) Division of work
**Answer: B** — Gang plank allows direct communication bypassing the chain.

**Q18.** Taylor and Fayol are respectively called:
(A) Father of management, Father of administration
(B) Father of scientific management, Father of general management
(C) Father of planning, Father of organizing
(D) Father of efficiency, Father of effectiveness
**Answer: B**

**Q19.** Functional foremanship requires a minimum of how many specialists?
(A) 4 (B) 6 (C) 8 (D) 10
**Answer: C** — 4 in planning (route clerk, instruction card clerk, time/cost clerk, disciplinarian) + 4 in production (speed boss, gang boss, repair boss, inspector).

**Q20.** Which principle emphasizes that there should be one plan for a group of activities having the same objective?
(A) Unity of command (B) Unity of direction (C) Division of work (D) Order
**Answer: B** — One head and one plan for each group of activities.

## Chapter 3: Business Environment (10 Qs)

**Q21.** Liberalization in India refers to:
(A) Increasing government control (B) Reducing trade barriers and regulations
(C) Nationalizing industries (D) Increasing tax rates
**Answer: B**

**Q22.** Which of the following is a component of the specific (micro) environment?
(A) Political conditions (B) Competitors (C) Technology (D) Social trends
**Answer: B** — Competitors, suppliers, customers are micro environment factors.

**Q23.** PEST analysis covers:
(A) Political, Economic, Social, Technological factors
(B) Personal, Environmental, Strategic, Tactical factors
(C) Planning, Evaluation, Strategy, Trends
(D) None of the above
**Answer: A**

**Q24.** Demonetization of ₹500 and ₹1000 notes in 2016 is an example of change in:
(A) Social environment (B) Legal environment (C) Economic/Political environment (D) Technological environment
**Answer: C**

**Q25.** GST implementation in India (2017) is an example of:
(A) Liberalization (B) Legal/Economic environment change (C) Globalization (D) Privatization
**Answer: B**

## Chapter 4-5: Planning & Organising (10 Qs)

**Q26.** Planning is:
(A) Looking ahead (B) Looking backward (C) A mental exercise (D) Both A and C
**Answer: D** — Planning is a mental exercise that involves looking ahead.

**Q27.** Standing plans include:
(A) Budget and programme (B) Policies, procedures, and rules
(C) Objectives and strategies (D) Methods and budgets
**Answer: B**

**Q28.** Which type of plan specifies step-by-step sequence for performing routine activities?
(A) Policy (B) Strategy (C) Procedure (D) Rule
**Answer: C**

**Q29.** Delegation of authority means:
(A) Transferring all responsibilities (B) Sharing decision-making power with subordinates
(C) Removing accountability (D) Centralizing control
**Answer: B**

**Q30.** Decentralization is an extension of:
(A) Division of work (B) Delegation (C) Unity of command (D) Span of management
**Answer: B**

## Chapter 6: Staffing (10 Qs)

**Q31.** The first step in the staffing process is:
(A) Selection (B) Recruitment (C) Estimating manpower requirements (D) Training
**Answer: C**

**Q32.** "Walk-in interview" is a method of:
(A) Internal recruitment (B) External recruitment (C) Selection (D) Training
**Answer: B**

**Q33.** Selection is a:
(A) Positive process (B) Negative process (C) Neutral process (D) Random process
**Answer: B** — Selection is negative as it involves rejection of unsuitable candidates.

**Q34.** On-the-job training methods include:
(A) Vestibule training (B) Apprenticeship (C) Classroom lectures (D) Case study method
**Answer: B**

**Q35.** Which of the following is NOT a step in the selection process?
(A) Preliminary screening (B) Employment interview (C) Job offer (D) Advertising vacancies
**Answer: D** — Advertising is part of recruitment, not selection.

## Chapter 7: Directing (10 Qs)

**Q36.** Maslow's hierarchy of needs places which need at the top?
(A) Safety needs (B) Esteem needs (C) Self-actualization needs (D) Social needs
**Answer: C**

**Q37.** According to Herzberg, which of the following is a "hygiene factor"?
(A) Recognition (B) Achievement (C) Salary/Working conditions (D) Growth
**Answer: C** — Hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction but don't motivate.

**Q38.** "Grapevine" communication is:
(A) Formal communication (B) Informal communication (C) Vertical communication (D) External communication
**Answer: B**

**Q39.** An autocratic leader:
(A) Shares decision-making (B) Takes all decisions without consulting subordinates
(C) Lets subordinates decide (D) Has no authority
**Answer: B**

**Q40.** Semantic barriers in communication arise due to:
(A) Physical distance (B) Differences in meaning/interpretation of words
(C) Organizational hierarchy (D) Emotional state
**Answer: B**

## Chapter 8: Controlling (10 Qs)

**Q41.** The correct sequence in the controlling process is:
(A) Set standards → Measure performance → Compare → Take corrective action
(B) Measure → Set standards → Compare → Correct
(C) Compare → Measure → Set → Correct
(D) Correct → Measure → Compare → Set
**Answer: A**

**Q42.** Management by Exception means:
(A) Managing all activities equally (B) Focusing only on significant deviations
(C) Delegating all control to subordinates (D) Avoiding control altogether
**Answer: B**

**Q43.** Budgetary control involves:
(A) Preparing budgets and comparing actual with budgeted
(B) Only preparing budgets
(C) Controlling only expenses
(D) None of the above
**Answer: A**

## Chapter 9-10: Financial Markets & Marketing (14 Qs)

**Q44.** SEBI was established in the year:
(A) 1988 (B) 1990 (C) 1992 (D) 1995
**Answer: A** — SEBI was established in 1988 and given statutory powers in 1992.

**Q45.** Commercial paper is:
(A) A long-term source of finance (B) An unsecured short-term promissory note
(C) Equity shares (D) Government bonds
**Answer: B**

**Q46.** The 4 Ps of marketing mix are:
(A) Product, Price, Place, Promotion
(B) Product, People, Process, Promotion
(C) Price, Place, People, Profit
(D) Product, Price, Profit, Place
**Answer: A**

**Q47.** Product life cycle has how many stages?
(A) 3 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 6
**Answer: B** — Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline.

**Q48.** "Personal selling" involves:
(A) Mass advertising (B) One-to-one interaction between seller and buyer
(C) Online marketing only (D) Sales promotion through discounts
**Answer: B**
