## DETAILED SOLUTIONS

**Q22 (C):** Let original = 100. After 20% increase = 120. After 20% decrease of 120 = 120 × 0.8 = 96. Net change = −4%.

**Q23 (B):** Sum = n(n+1)/2 = 50×51/2 = 1275.

**Q24 (B):** √(144×81) = √144 × √81 = 12 × 9 = 108.

**Q25 (B):** Profit% = (1000−800)/800 × 100 = 200/800 × 100 = 25%.

**Q26 (B):** 40% of x = 160 → x = 400. 75% of 400 = 300.

**Q27 (A):** SP = 500 × 0.9 = 450. Profit = 20%, so CP = 450/1.2 = ₹375.

**Q28 (A):** 3x and 5x. (3x+8)/(5x+8) = 5/7 → 21x+56 = 25x+40 → 4x = 16 → x = 4. Numbers: 12 and 20.

**Q29 (A):** Sum of 5 = 42×5 = 210. Sum of remaining 4 = 38×4 = 152. Excluded = 210−152 = 58.

**Q30 (A):** Milk = 4x, Water = x. (4x)/(x+5) = 4/3 → 12x = 4x+20 → 8x = 20 → x = 2.5. Milk = 10L.

**Q31 (B):** Distance = 200+300 = 500m. Speed = 500/25 = 20 m/s = 20×3.6 = 72 km/h.

**Q32 (B):** Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/15 = (5+4)/60 = 9/60 = 3/20. Days = 20/3 = 6⅔ days.

**Q33 (C):** Upstream speed = 10−2 = 8 km/h. Time = 48/8 = 6 hours.

**Q34 (B):** Increase = (250−180)/180 × 100 = 70/180 × 100 ≈ 38.9%.

**Q35 (B):** Average = (150+180+200+250+300)/5 = 1080/5 = ₹216 crore.

**Q36 (C):** Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Next = 30+12 = 42.

**Q37 (B):** Differences: 5, 7, 9, 11, 13. Next = 35+13 = 48.

**Q38 (C):** Gaps: +2, +3, +4, +5. J + 5 = O.

**Q51 (A):** Pattern: (a+b) × a = result. 2+3: 5×2=10. 4+5: 9×4=36. 6+7: 13×6=78. 8+9: 17×8=136.

**Q52 (B):** Each row contains squares of consecutive odd numbers. Row 3: 6²=36, 10²=100, 15²=225.

**Q55 (B):** At 3:15, minute hand at 90°, hour hand at 90° + 7.5° (15 min × 0.5°/min) = 97.5°. Angle = 7.5°.
