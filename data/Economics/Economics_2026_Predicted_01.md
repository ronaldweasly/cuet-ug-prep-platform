## DETAILED EXPLANATIONS

**Q1 (B):** On the PPC, all resources are fully and efficiently utilized. Points inside represent underutilization, points outside are unattainable.

**Q5 (A):** Consumer equilibrium in cardinal utility: MUx/Px = MUy/Py = MUm (marginal utility of money).

**Q7 (C):** MUa/Pa = 40/10 = 4; MUb/Pb = 60/20 = 3. Wait — actually MUa/Pa > MUb/Pb, so consumer should buy more of A and less of B. **Corrected: A**

**Q10 (C):** When TP is maximum, MP = 0. After this point, MP becomes negative and TP starts falling.

**Q12 (A):** AFC = TFC/Q = 500/100 = ₹5 per unit.

**Q14 (C):** When AC is falling, MC must be below AC (MC pulls AC down).

**Q15 (A):** Total Cost = (10×500) + (5×1000) = 5000 + 5000 = ₹10,000. AC = TC/Q = 10000/200 = ₹50.

**Q18 (B):** Profit = (P − ATC) × Q = (20 − 15) × 100 = ₹500 super-normal profit.

**Q35 (A):** NNP at FC = GDP at MP − Depreciation + NFIA − NIT = 5000 − 300 + 200 − 400 = ₹4,500 crore.

**Q40 (B):** Total credit = Initial deposit × (1/LRR) = 10,000 × (1/0.20) = ₹50,000.

**Q42 (B):** MPC = 0.8, so Multiplier = 1/(1−MPC) = 1/(1−0.8) = 1/0.2 = 5.

**Q43 (B):** Multiplier = 1/(1−0.75) = 4. ΔY = K × ΔI = 4 × 200 = ₹800 crore.

**Q48 (A):** Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments. It shows borrowing needs excluding interest obligations.
