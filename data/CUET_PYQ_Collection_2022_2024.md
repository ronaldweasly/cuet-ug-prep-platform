## BUSINESS STUDIES PYQs

### CUET 2022

**Q1.** Controlling function of an organisation is ________.
- (A) Forward looking
- (B) Backward looking
- (C) Both forward and backward looking
- (D) Neither forward nor backward looking
**Answer: (C)** Both forward and backward looking
*Source: examrace.com, targetpublications.org*

**Q2.** Quantitative standards should be set so that it is easier to match the actual performance with the desired (planned) performance. Identify the function of management discussed above.
- (A) Planning
- (B) Controlling
- (C) Staffing
- (D) Directing
**Answer: (B)** Controlling
*Source: examrace.com*

**Q3.** 'In a bulb manufacturing unit, the range of defective bulb is decided at 5%. Any deviation that goes beyond the permissible limit of defect is to be brought to the notice of supervisor'. Identify the concept.
- (A) Control by Exception
- (B) Critical Point Control
- (C) Controlling
- (D) Key Result Areas
**Answer: (A)** Control by Exception
*Source: targetpublications.org*

**Q4.** Which of the following is not a limitation of controlling?
- (A) Difficulty in setting quantitative standards
- (B) Resistance from employees
- (C) Ensuring order and discipline
- (D) Costly affair
**Answer: (C)** Ensuring order and discipline (this is an advantage, not limitation)
*Source: targetpublications.org*

**Q5.** Vasu has been working as a junior clerk for five years. Instead of promoting Vasu, the company appointed Raman through a placement agency. Vasu felt demotivated. Identify the limitation of external recruitment discussed above.
- (A) Lengthy process
- (B) Dissatisfaction among existing staff
- (C) Danger of inbreeding
- (D) Competition spirit is hampered
**Answer: (B)** Dissatisfaction among existing staff
*Source: targetpublications.org*

### CUET 2024

**Q6.** Identify the correct sequence of steps under the Planning function:
- (A) Setting objectives → Developing premises → Identifying alternatives → Evaluating alternatives
- (B) Developing premises → Setting objectives → Evaluating → Identifying
- (C) Identifying alternatives → Setting objectives → Evaluating → Developing premises
- (D) Evaluating → Identifying → Setting objectives → Developing premises
**Answer: (A)** Setting objectives → Developing premises → Identifying alternatives → Evaluating alternatives
*Source: collegedunia.com, zollege.in*

**Q7.** Match List-I (Fayol's Principles) with List-II:
| List-I | List-II |
|--------|---------|
| (A) Obedience to rules | (I) Unity of Direction |
| (B) One boss per employee | (II) Remuneration |
| (C) One head, one plan | (III) Discipline |
| (D) Fair remuneration | (IV) Unity of Command |
**Answer:** (A)-(III), (B)-(IV), (C)-(I), (D)-(II)
*Source: prepp.in, zollege.in*

**Q8.** Taylor pioneered scientific inquiry in management practice. Identify this principle.
**Answer:** Science, not Rule of Thumb
*Source: zollege.in, toprankers.com*

**Q9.** The aim of which Taylor technique is to reduce a product to fixed types, sizes, and characteristics?
**Answer:** Standardisation of work
*Source: zollege.in*

**Q10.** What is the primary objective of financial management?
- (1) Increase shareholders' wealth
- (2) Decrease shareholders' wealth
- (3) Increase shareholders' capital
- (4) Decrease shareholders' capital
**Answer: (1)** Increase shareholders' wealth
*Source: collegedunia.com*

**Q11.** "Consumers form an important group among stakeholders and their interest has to be well taken care of." Identify the importance of consumer protection.
- (1) Moral justification
- (2) Business uses society's resources
- (3) Social responsibility
- (4) Long-term interest of business
**Answer: (3)** Social responsibility
*Source: collegedunia.com, afterboards.in*

**Q12.** Under which dimension is "Online Booking of Air Tickets" included?
- (A) Legal (B) Economic (C) Technical (D) Social
**Answer: (C)** Technical dimension
*Source: CUET pattern*

**Q13.** The market where existing securities are bought and sold:
- (A) Primary market (B) Secondary market (C) Capital market (D) Financial market
**Answer: (B)** Secondary market

**Q14.** Which is NOT an element of delegation?
- (a) Responsibility (b) Authority (c) Accountability (d) Decentralisation
**Answer: (d)** Decentralisation
*Source: CUET pattern*

**Q15.** Arrange organising process steps: A. Establishing authority, B. Identification of work, C. Departmentalisation, D. Assignment of duties
**Answer:** B, C, D, A
*Source: CUET pattern*

**Q16.** Grouping of activities on the basis of product lines is part of:
- (a) Functional organisation (b) Divisional organisation (c) Centralised (d) Informal
**Answer: (b)** Divisional organisation

## ECONOMICS PYQs

### CUET 2024

**Q1.** The value of the investment multiplier is determined by:
- (1) Inverse of SLR
- (2) Inverse of MPS
- (3) Gold reserves with central bank
- (4) Savings with public
**Answer: (2)** Inverse of MPS (K = 1/MPS)
*Source: cuetmock.com, afterboards.in*

**Q2.** If the investment multiplier is 5 and increased income is ₹800 crore, find the change in investment.
- (1) ₹4000 crore (2) ₹120 crore (3) ₹400 crore (4) ₹160 crore
**Answer: (4)** ₹160 crore (K = ΔY/ΔI → 5 = 800/ΔI → ΔI = 160)
*Source: zollege.in, afterboards.in*

**Q3.** MPS and investment multiplier are:
- (1) Directly related (2) Inversely related (3) MPC & multiplier inversely related (4) No relation
**Answer: (2)** Inversely related
*Source: collegedunia.com*

**Q4.** Which is NOT a measure used by RBI to control money supply?
- (1) Bank Rate (2) Open Market Operations (3) Fiscal Spending (4) Reserve Ratios
**Answer: (3)** Fiscal Spending (this is a government/fiscal policy tool)

**Q5.** Which is NOT a function of commercial banks?
- (1) Accepting deposits (2) Offering loans (3) Overdraft facilities (4) Fixing CRR
**Answer: (4)** Fixing CRR (done by RBI, not commercial banks)

**Q6.** Government Budget is:
- (1) Balance of Payments (2) Annual financial statement (3) Monetary Policy (4) Five-Year Plan
**Answer: (2)** Annual financial statement of the government

**Q7.** Fiscal deficit can prove inflationary if financed by:
- (1) Borrowing from public (2) Printing new currency (3) Disinvestment (4) External grants
**Answer: (2)** Printing of new currency

**Q8.** When Ed = 1 at every point, the demand curve is:
- (A) Perfectly inelastic (B) Perfectly elastic (C) Rectangular Hyperbola (D) > Unitary
**Answer: (C)** Rectangular Hyperbola

**Q9.** If consumer preferences change in favor of a good, demand curve:
- (A) Shifts rightward (B) Shifts leftward (C) Moves up along curve (D) Moves down
**Answer: (A)** Shifts rightward
