## Instructions for Collection

1. **Official Priority**: Start with NTA official portal and CUET official candidate portal
2. **Verify Authenticity**: Check publication date and ensure 2022+ papers
3. **Check for Answer Keys**: Prefer papers that include solutions
4. **Avoid Duplicates**: Compare paper content before storing
5. **Test Validity**: Ensure PDFs are readable and content is relevant
