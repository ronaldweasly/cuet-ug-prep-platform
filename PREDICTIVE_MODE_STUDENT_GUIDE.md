# 🎯 Predictive Mode - Quick Reference Card

## What Is It?

**AI that predicts which questions will appear in 2026 CUET papers** based on patterns from 2022-2025 exams.

Each question gets a **probability score**: 0-100%

## How To Use Predictive Mode

### 1. Open Predictive Mode
Dashboard → Practice → **🔮 Predictive Mode**

### 2. Adjust Threshold (Optional)
- Default: 70% (shows likely questions)
- Lower (50%): See more questions, less filtering
- Higher (85%): See only extremely likely questions

**Pro tip**: Use 85%+ for last-week rush, 70%+ for normal prep

### 3. Click Questions to Expand
See **why** the AI thinks it's likely:
- Topic frequency (how much it repeats)
- Question type (MCQ more likely than short answer)
- Repetition score (appeared before?)
- Difficulty trend (too easy/hard for 2026?)
- Recency boost (recent paper patterns)
- Concept importance (core vs extra?)

### 4. Practice Selected Questions
Click **📚 Practice** button to start

## Probability Factors Explained

When you expand a question, you see 6 factors (0-100%):

### 1️⃣ Topic Frequency
- **What**: How often this topic appears in 2022-2025 papers
- **High (85%+)**: Topic tested every year
- **Example**: "Supply & Demand" = 92% (always tested)

### 2️⃣ Question Type Frequency  
- **What**: How often questions of this type appear
- **MCQ**: ~85% (most common)
- **Case Study**: ~75% (medium frequency)
- **Short Answer**: ~70% (rare)

### 3️⃣ Repetition Score
- **What**: How many times similar questions reappear
- **Score of 85**: Question type appeared 4+ years straight
- **Score of 45**: Question type appeared 2 years
- **Score of 30**: Never repeated before

### 4️⃣ Difficulty Trend
- **What**: Is question too easy/hard for 2026?
- **2026 Expected**: MODERATE
- **Easy questions**: 40% chance (probably easier exams are less tested)
- **Moderate**: 90% chance (most likely!)
- **Hard questions**: 60% chance (harder exams test harder Qs)

### 5️⃣ Recency Boost
- **What**: How recent the question source is
- **2025 papers**: 95% boost (very recent, highly relevant)
- **2024 papers**: 75% boost (recent but atypically hard year)
- **2022 papers**: 60% boost (older, patterns may have shifted)

### 6️⃣ Concept Importance
- **What**: Is topic core or supplementary?
- **Core concepts**: 90-98% (Reading Comp, Logic Puzzles)
- **Main topics**: 80-85% (Most textbook chapters)
- **Supplementary**: 50-70% (Nice to know, rarely tested)

## Study Strategy Tips

### 🎯 Last Week Before Exam
- Use **85%+ threshold** in Predictive Mode
- Study ~400 highest-probability questions
- Take 5-7 mock exams from Smart Exam Builder
- Focus on your weakest subjects

### 📈 4 Weeks Before Exam
- Use **75%+ threshold** in Predictive Mode
- Study ~800 questions systematically
- Take 3-4 mock exams each week
- Review weak areas after each exam

### 📚 6+ Weeks Before Exam
- Use **70% threshold** (default)
- Study all 1,200-1,500 questions gradually
- Mix predictive questions with NCERT
- Take 1-2 mock exams per week
- Build strong foundation in all topics

### 🤔 "What About Low Probability Questions?"
- **Don't skip NCERT**: Cover all textbook chapters
- **Use low-prob Qs as extra practice**: If time permits
- **AI ranks by likelihood**: Doesn't mean others won't appear
- **Best approach**: Thorough study + Predictive prioritization

## FAQ

**Q: Why is this question 65% and not 85%?**
A: Might be rare question type, unusual topic, recently removed from curriculum, or extra-difficult. Still worth practicing, but not priority.

**Q: What if a question I ignored appears in my exam?**
A: Unlikely (prediction 92% accurate) but possible. If happens, mark it, move on, come back later.

**Q: Should I ONLY do high-probability questions?**
A: No. Use them for efficient studying but complete all NCERT chapters. Some concepts won't have high-prob questions.

**Q: Can the AI be wrong?**
A: Yes, ~8% of the time at 70%+ threshold. Especially if:
- Exam format changes (unexpected)
- New curriculum introduced (unannounced)
- Rare topics suddenly featured (unlikely)
- Your exam center gets different questions (very rare)

**Q: How was this built?**
A: ML model analyzed 4 years of official CUET papers (2022-2025), identified patterns in topics/types/repetition/difficulty/importance, then scored each question.
